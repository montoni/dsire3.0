State energy policy trackers

Interactive maps and searchable databases of state legislative and regulatory actions, built on DSIRE Insight data. Pick a topic to explore what each state is doing.

Trackers

  • Energy affordability

    Utility bill and rate actions, cost allocation, large load tariffs, and customer assistance programs.

  • Distributed solar

    Net metering, community solar, and rate design policies for customer-sited solar.

  • Grid modernization

    Grid planning, utility business models, advanced metering, and distribution system actions.

  • Electric vehicles

    Charging infrastructure, EV rates, utility programs, and transportation electrification.

  • Power decarbonization

    Clean energy standards, emissions targets, and resource planning for the power sector.

  • State incentives for renewables and efficiency

    Rebates, tax credits, loans, and other incentives for renewable energy and energy efficiency.
